Support Center

Everything you need to know about match day.

How do I register a team?

Registration is handled via Stripe. Click the "Register Team" button on the homepage to pay the entry fee. Once paid, you'll receive an email to submit your roster.

What is the squad size?

The tournament is 7-a-side. You can register up to 10 players per team (7 starters + 3 subs). Rolling substitutions are allowed throughout the game.

What happens if it rains?

We play on high-quality 3G pitches at Castle Green, which are all-weather surfaces. The tournament proceeds rain or shine unless conditions are deemed dangerous by officials.

What is the refund policy?

Entry fees are non-refundable to ensure commitment and fixture stability. However, if FixUp Arena cancels the event, 100% of fees will be refunded immediately.
